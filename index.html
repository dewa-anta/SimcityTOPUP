FAQ

Questions before you order

Q1What do I need to send?

Just your City ID from SimCity BuildIt (Options → City ID) and the items you'd like. No password or personal login is ever required.

Q2How long does delivery take?

Most orders are confirmed and delivered within minutes of payment. Larger orders may take a little longer during peak hours.

Q3What payment methods do you accept?

We accept PayPal and Remitly. You'll pick your preferred method at checkout, and full payment details are shared directly in WhatsApp once you send your order.

Q4Is my account safe?

Yes. We never ask for your EA/game account password — only your public City ID, which is safe to share.
